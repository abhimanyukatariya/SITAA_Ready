SITAA First Cohort Announced

Under the Scheme for Innovation and Technology Association with Aadhaar (SITAA), the first cohort has been announced across three focused ID-Tech innovation challenges. The selected participants will contribute towards indigenous, scalable and secure solutions for strengthening India’s Digital Public Infrastructure and the Aadhaar ecosystem.
